1.What Amplified Thinker is

Amplified Thinker is a library of primers, plans and articles on workplace skills, grounded in published research and in practice. Anyone can read all of it without an account. Creating an account adds three things: the site remembers your position in the primers and plans you open, you can mark them complete, and one page shows your progress across all of them. The service is provided free of charge.

2.Your account

An account is optional. What it holds and how it is handled is set out in the privacy notice; what it is for is on why sign up.

  • Use an email address you control, and give accurate details. Signing up sends a link that activates the account, and you cannot sign in until you have followed it.
  • You are responsible for keeping your password secure and for what happens under your account. Tell us promptly if you think someone else has access to it.
  • You must be at least 13 years old to create an account.
  • Two emails come with the account and cannot be switched off — the confirmation and a password reset — because without them the account cannot be used. Updates about new skills and features are separate and opt-in: choose at sign-up, change it on your account page, or use the unsubscribe link in any of them. Turning those off never affects the two above.
  • If you think someone else is in your account, reset your password from the sign-in page — that also signs out every other device — and let us know.

3.Using the material

You may read everything here, use it in your own work, and share it with colleagues. You do not need permission and you do not need an account. A few things are asked in return:

  • No bulk copying. Automated scraping, mirroring the site, or harvesting it wholesale for another product or dataset is not permitted.
